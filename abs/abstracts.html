Making the abstract concrete

snrXvAI as implemented only feeds snarXiv-generated titles to the AIs. You might wonder what nonsense ensues if they are given snarXiv-generated abstracts as well. To a trained non-incompetent human[1], these are trivially easy to distinguish from genuine abstracts, by virtue of the pleasant sensation of mental disorientation one gets from reading them - whereas real abstracts give an unpleasant sensation of mental disorientation[2].

Title: Analyzing an Instanton

In short, recently, work on a Landau-Ginzburg model has opened up a leading class of Planck models. Inspired by this, we find evidence for type IIB. Our results are similar to work done by Witten.
