Executive Verdict

Short answer: None of the eight papers produces a genuine, publishable hep-th research breakthrough (unsurprising given a nonsensical snarXiv abstract). However, one paper — claude-fable-5-2_high_1 — is in a completely different league from the other seven. Rather than just pasting textbook formulas or hallucinating formal theorems, it genuinely derives and verifies several concrete, non-trivial exact identities and numerical tests.

claude-opus-5-5_high_1 is a distant second: intellectually honest and clean, with a couple of elementary-but-valid mini-results. The remaining six range from honest textbook reviews or explicit disclaimers (claude-sonnet-5-5, claude-haiku-5-5) to glossy hep-th pastiche containing either contrived bridges over solid textbook appendices (gemini-4-neon) or outright mathematical and physical hallucinations (gemini-4-hydrogen, gemini-4-argon, gemini-3.8-flash).

1. The Standout: claude-fable-5-2_high_1

runs/claude-fable-5-2_high_1/analyzing_an_instanton.tex Claude 1,081 lines · Verified

Rather than pretending orientifold planes float in the Milky Way, claude-fable-5-2 opens with an explicit dictionary (Table 1) translating each snarXiv phrase into a well-defined mathematical object, clearly separates exact propositions from reciprocity assumptions and numerical checks, and performs six genuinely impressive, concrete calculations:

1. Explicit d-Dimensional Reduction of the Level-1 Yangian Operator for Contact Witten Diagrams Props. 2–3 (L265–310), App. A (L654–671)

Lifting n-point contact Witten diagrams (instanton amplitudes) to conformal star integrals in D=∑iΔi dimensions is known (it cites Rigatos & Zhou, PRL 2022), but the paper goes further and explicitly computes the restriction of the D-dimensional level-1 Yangian momentum generator to the physical d-dimensional subspace, isolating the exact transverse mismatch term:

[ P^(d)μ + i ( ∑k=1nΔk −d ) ∑i<j xijμ ∂ ∂xij2 ] An =0.
Independently verified (SymPy): Evaluated symbolically on the 3-point dilaton amplitude (d=4, Δi=(4,4,4), D=12): the two pieces give +683/7239213000000 and -683/7239213000000, canceling identically to 0. Arguably the neatest single calculation in the entire batch.
2. Three-Line Gegenbauer Proof of the AdS5 × S5 Propagator Decomposition Prop. 1 (L194–214)

Proves the exact partial-wave expansion of the 10D massless D-instanton propagator by applying (1sinhσ∂σ)2 directly to the Gegenbauer generating function:

1(coshσ−cosθ)4 = 16π23 ∑ℓ=0∞ (ℓ+2) Gℓ+4(σ) Cℓ(2)(cosθ).

Known physics (Caron-Huot & Trinh / Bianchi–Green–Kovacs–Rossi), derived with unusual economy and exact numerical constants.

3. Strong-Coupling Time-Like Anomalous Dimensions at General S5 Harmonic ℓ Sec. 4.1 (L368–386)

Applies Basso–Korchemsky reciprocity γT(j)=γS(j−γT(j)) at fixed ℓ to the Brower–Polchinski–Strassler–Tan Pomeron trajectory, yielding:

γT(j,ℓ) = (j−2) (1−j+22λ) + ℓ(ℓ+4)2λ.

Reproduces Hatta & Matsuo (2008) at ℓ=0, predicts R-charge-weighted energy-flow diffusion on S5 as D2,ℓ(Q2)∝Q−CS(ℓ)/(2λ), and transparently flags fixed-ℓ reciprocity as an open assumption.

4. Universal Linear Freund–Rubin Spectrum for All (m, n) Prop. 4 (L404–419)

Proves that in any Freund–Rubin background AdSm×Sn, the Einstein-equation radius ratio L/R=(m−1)/(n−1) makes the mass-shell quadratic factorize universally as Δ^(Δ^−1)=ℓ^(ℓ^+1), giving:

Δm−1 = ℓn−1 +1.

A sharp contrast with the other models: gemini-4-neon called the perfect-square discriminant for (5,5), (4,7), (7,4) an “algebraic miracle” of maximally supersymmetric cases, and claude-opus-5-5 erroneously stated that (4,7) and (7,4) don't yield integers/rationals. Only claude-fable-5-2 spotted the general algebraic identity.

5. Axion WGC + Kaloper–Sorbo Massive 3-Form Dark Energy Bounds Sec. 5.2–5.3 (L503–584)

Combines the dilation-invariant axion relation faS=rτ=b/2MP with Dvali/Kaloper–Sorbo 3-form monodromy (“longitudinal dark energy,” 2πfaμ=q0) to eliminate fa via μ=q0S/(2πrτ), deriving concrete quantitative bounds: S≳289, gs≲0.022, at least 92 windings, and q0≲0.28meV.

6. High-Precision Duality & Hessian Test of E3/2(τ, τ̄) at Rigid LG Elliptic Points Sec. 6, Table 3 & L602–641

Grounds “recent work on a Landau–Ginzburg model” in real recent literature (Becker–Gonzalo–Walcher–Wrase, JHEP 2022, rigid LG flux vacua stabilizing τ at the elliptic points τsq=i and τhex=eiπ/3). Shows numerically that adding k=1…4 D-instantons drives the perturbative stationarity violation from O(1) down to 4.2×10−11 and −2.8×10−9; shows replacing the divisor measure νk=∑d|kd−2 by 1 breaks convergence by 6–7 orders of magnitude; shows instantons flip the perturbative ordering to E3/2(τhex)<E3/2(τsq) (matching Montgomery 1988); and fixes the exact isotropic Hessian at τhex from ℤ3 symmetry + the Laplace equation (∂12E=∂22E=12E(τhex)=4.4463726) while showing τsq=i is a saddle (−1.98389 vs. +8.75911).

Independently verified (mpmath, 25 digits): E32(i) = 9.033621683, E32(exp(i*pi/3)) = 8.8927451, Hessian at i = (-1.983891, +8.759108). Every printed digit in the paper is exact.

3. Meta-Observation: The Claude vs. Gemini Behavioral Split

Claude Models: Semantic Reframing

All four Claude models immediately recognized the snarXiv abstract as literal nonsense and responded by redefining the absurd phrases via an explicit upfront glossary (or disclaiming the prompt altogether, in Haiku's case).

That methodological choice freed claude-fable-5-2 to treat the abstract as a formal constraint puzzle and pack genuine, self-consistent, independently verifiable mathematical physics into every section.

Gemini Models: Literal Compliance

All four Gemini models took the snarXiv phrases literally — placing physical orientifold planes inside the magnetized galactic ISM (ne~10−2cm−3, BISM~μG) and connecting primordial UHECR cascades directly to inflation at MPl.

Even when the strongest one (gemini-4-neon) proved real integrable-systems and 11D supergravity identities in its appendices, literal compliance forced its cross-section bridge theorems to become contrived or vacuous, and pushed the weaker models into fabricated math.
