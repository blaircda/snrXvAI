Degenerating hep-th papers

snarXiv vs arXiv ... vs snrXvAI

Consider the set of all possible hep-th paper titles. A plausible subset of this can be generated using David Simmons-Duffin's snarXiv, a recursive grammar for the cramped prose of high energy physics. Compared to the generative possibilities of Language Models Of Unusual Size, this effort may nowadays seem charming, even quaint[1].

However, thanks to these very same marvels of modern technology, anyone with a smattering of dollars in their account can bring the snarXiv to life[2] in AI-(de)generated form.

How snrXvAI?

I send snarXiv-generated titles to a collection of AI models and ask them to write[3] a paper, ideally one that makes sense, based on that title. I then post the results as a listing here. There could be genuine results in these papers; or the authors could be in on the joke[4].

I don't keep past listings. Systematics experiments are left as an exercise.
